How to Play

The Story

Pepe, a brave wanderer, got lost deep in the scorching Mexican desert. With nothing but his courage and a handful of salsa bottles, he must battle through hordes of crazy chickens and face the mighty Endboss a giant, furious rooster guarding the only way home.

Collect coins for glory, grab bottles to throw at enemies, and jump on chickens to squash them. But beware the Endboss won't go down easily!
